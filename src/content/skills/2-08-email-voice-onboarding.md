## What It Does

Email Voice Onboarding captures your personal email writing style by analyzing your real sent emails — greeting patterns, tone, vocabulary, sentence structure, and how you adapt across audiences. It produces a 9-section Communication Profile that AI uses to draft emails that sound authentically like you.

## How It Works

1. **Gather your info** — name, title, role, who you email most
2. **Pull your sent emails** — 30–50 emails across 3–6 months via Outlook
3. **Analyze your voice** — extract tone, vocabulary, structure, and context patterns
4. **Build your profile** — 9-section Communication Profile with TLH context pre-populated
5. **Set up self-improvement** — corrections log that learns from your edits over time
6. **Create your runtime skill** — personalized email-voice skill that triggers on "draft", "reply", "compose"
7. **Schedule maintenance** — monthly refresh + daily inbox summaries

## What You Get

Your personalized files are saved to your AI Tools folder:

- **Communication Profile** — your complete voice fingerprint (tone, vocabulary, structure, rules, examples)
- **Runtime Skill** — triggers whenever you ask to draft or reply to an email
- **Corrections Log** — learns from every edit you make, so drafts improve over time
